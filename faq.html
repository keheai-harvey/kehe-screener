Frequently asked questions

Is the sanctions check really free?

Yes. The instant check against OFAC, UK, EU, UN and US BIS lists runs entirely in your browser; we never see the names you check, and no account or card is needed.

What is the difference between Quick Check and the full report?

Quick Check ($19) is a sanctions screen plus a registration snapshot, delivered as a summary in 24h. The full report ($100) adds beneficial ownership layers, litigation and enforcement deep-dive, and bilingual filing copy.

What does the China Supplier Verification Report include?

For $120 we check official Chinese public records: registration and operating status, judgment-debtor blacklist, administrative penalties, and published litigation, delivered as an English report within 72 hours.

How fast is delivery?

Quick Check and the full report are delivered within 24 hours; China supplier reports within 72 hours, by email.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes — 7-day refund if the report doesn't add value, no forms.

Does a clean screen mean the counterparty is safe?

No. A clean screen only means no exact or close match on those four sanctions lists. Registration, ownership, litigation and reputation are separate checks — that's what the paid reports cover.

Are the reports legal opinions?

No. Reports are plain-English compilations of official public records for risk-awareness, not legal or credit opinions.
