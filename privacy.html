Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

1. What we collect

When you use the free sanctions screener, your search stays in your browser and is not sent to us. If you submit our request form, we collect the company name you ask about, your email address, and the message you write. If you place an order, Creem (our payment provider) collects your payment details — we never see or store your card details.

2. How we use it

We use the information you submit only to prepare the verification report you requested and to reply to you. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with anyone, except as required to deliver the service (for example, our payment provider) or by law.

3. Where we store it

Form submissions are received at our email inbox in China. Reports are delivered by email. Data is retained only as long as needed to complete and document your request.

4. Cookies

This site does not use tracking cookies or advertising cookies. We use the Sanctions list data and any analytics only in aggregated, non-identifying form.

5. Your rights

You may ask us to correct or delete the personal data you submitted at any time by replying to your confirmation email or contacting us through the form on the screener page.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy? Use the contact form on the screener page.
