## Deliverability checklist
1. SPF + DKIM set (use a provider like Resend with a verified domain — don't send from a free inbox)
2. Warm up a new domain 2 weeks before volume
3. Keep volume under ~30/day per domain (human rhythm)
4. Rotate 3+ subject/body variants — never blast the same template
5. Include a one-click unsubscribe and physical address footer
6. Monitor bounce rate (<3%) and complaint rate (<0.1%) — stop if flagged
7. Never buy lists; build opt-in or targeted lists only
