## Title formula (Amazon)
`[Brand] + [Core keyword] + [Benefit/differentiator] + [Key attribute] + [Use case]`

Example:
> AcmeLite LED Headlamp Rechargeable 800 Lumen, Waterproof, Motion Sensor, 8H Runtime — for Camping, Hiking, Running

Rule: put your main keyword at the front (title weight), keep it readable, front-load the differentiator. Avoid keyword stuffing — Amazon ranks natural readability over repetition now.

## 5 bullet templates
1. **Benefit-first**: "X lets you [outcome] without [pain]. [One-line proof]."
2. **Spec-backed**: "Rated [spec] — [what that means in everyday use]."
3. **Objection-killer**: "Worried about [objection]? [How the product solves it]."
4. **Use-case**: "Perfect for [use case 1], [use case 2], and [use case 3]."
5. **Social proof / guarantee**: "[Rating]+ stars from [N]+ customers. [Guarantee/warranty]."

## Description structure A — Story
1. Open with the customer's moment of pain (2-3 lines, second person)
2. Introduce the product as the fix
3. 3-5 short paragraphs: what it is, why it's better, what's included
4. Close with guarantee + CTA

## Description structure B — Comparison
Table format: the problem → competitors' usual approach → how ours is different.

## SP ad copy pack
Headlines:
- "The [category] That [outcome]"
- "[Benefit] Without [Pain]"
- "Built For [Specific Audience]"
- "Try It 30 Days Risk-Free"

Ad body (5):
1. Problem → solution → guarantee → CTA
2. "Why 10,000+ customers switched to [brand]"
3. "Still using [old way]? Here's why that's costing you [cost]."
4. "New arrival: [product] — [1-line differentiator]."
5. "Bundle & save: [deal] ends [date]."

## Keyword research (search-first)
1. Amazon autocomplete: type your core word, harvest 20+ long-tails (they're buyer intent)
2. Search result titles: what phrases repeat in top 10 listings = what ranks
3. Filter: relevance > volume; pick long-tails under competition
4. Place: main keyword in title + first bullet + backend search terms

## Listing health checklist
- [ ] Title under 180 chars, main keyword first
- [ ] First 2 bullets answer "why buy" not "what is"
- [ ] Images show scale/use (not just studio shots)
- [ ] A+ content used (story/comparison)
- [ ] Backend search terms filled (no brand names of others)
- [ ] Price vs competition checked (not too high without differentiation)
- [ ] Reviews strategy in place (insert cards, follow-up emails)
