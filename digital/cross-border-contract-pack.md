## 1. International Sales Contract (outline)
1. Parties (full legal names, addresses, tax IDs)
2. Product & specifications (attachment: spec sheet, samples)
3. Quantity & units
4. Price & currency (fixed vs adjustable; who pays bank fees)
5. **Incoterms 2020** (FOB/CIF/DDP — where risk & cost transfer)
6. Payment terms (T/T deposit %, L/C, escrow; due dates)
7. Delivery & shipping (dates, carrier, documents required)
8. Inspection & quality (pre-shipment inspection, standards, tolerances)
9. **Penalty for delay** (e.g., 0.5%/day, cap 10%)
10. Warranty & returns (defects window, remedy options)
11. Force majeure
12. Governing law & **arbitration** (CIETAC / HKIAC / ICC — pick one)
13. Signatures & authorization

## 2. China Supplier Supply Agreement (key clauses)
- **Deposit cap**: ≤30% deposit, balance against documents (B/L) or inspection
- **QC clause**: independent inspection right (SGS/BV/CCIC) before shipment
- **Defect penalty**: replacement at supplier cost + compensation cap
- **IP clause**: no unauthorized use of buyer's marks/designs
- **Dispute**: arbitration in [China/HK], not local court roulette
- **Term & notice**: renewal, termination notice 30-60 days

## 3. Mutual NDA (short form)
- Definition of confidential info (exclude public, independently developed)
- Obligations: use-only-for-purpose, no disclosure, no reverse engineering
- Term: 2-5 years + survival clause
- Remedies: injunction + damages
- Governing law + signature blocks

## 4. Agency / Distribution Agreement
- Territory & exclusivity (or non-exclusive)
- Commission rate & calculation (net of taxes/shipping)
- Reporting & minimum targets (with ramp-up)
- Trademark & marketing responsibilities
- Termination & post-term stock handling

## 5. Proforma Invoice (fields)
- Seller/buyer legal info, VAT/tax IDs
- Line items: description, HS code, quantity, unit price, total
- Incoterms, port of loading/discharge
- Payment terms & bank details
- Validity date, estimated shipment date

## 6. Key-clause cheat sheet
- Always define: Incoterms, payment trigger, inspection point, arbitration seat, penalty cap, governing law
- Never ship without: written PO, agreed spec, inspection right, dispute clause
- Deposit rule of thumb: 30% max, or escrow for first orders
